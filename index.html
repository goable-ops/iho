The problem

Every whanau trust faces the same walls.

5,400 trusts across Aotearoa. Most operating on paper, email and memory. Knowledge disappearing with every kaumatua who passes. Iho was built because we lived this from the inside.

No transparency
Beneficiaries have no visibility of land holdings, financial decisions or governance activity.
No communication
Whanau are disconnected from their own trust. News travels by word of mouth or not at all.
No financial visibility
Unclaimed dividends sitting with agencies for years. Nobody knows they exist.
Knowledge loss
Critical history lives in one kaumatua's memory. When they pass, decades of knowledge goes with them.
No governance tools
Paper files, email chains and spreadsheets. No single source of truth for trustees or beneficiaries.
No grant access
Trusts do not know what funding exists for them or how to find and apply for it.

Te Whare

Your digital whare. Everything in one place.

Each Iho portal is completely private and isolated. Only your trustees and approved whanau can access it. No shared infrastructure. No other trust can see your information.

Whakapapa Records
Living lineage documentation preserved and accessible across generations. Your whakapapa held safely for your tamariki and mokopuna.
Land Holdings Dashboard
Clear visibility of all land blocks, ownership interests, share holdings and succession status linked to the Maori Land Court.
Document Vault
Trust orders, title documents, legal records, court correspondence and historical papers stored securely and searchable.
Governance Hub
Meeting minutes, trustee resolutions, annual reports and governance decisions. A complete and searchable record of your trust's history.
Korero and Whakaaro
A private space for trustees and whanau to communicate, share news, raise ideas and stay connected to the collective.
Photo Gallery and Taonga
Preserve and share whanau photographs, cultural taonga, oral histories and memories across every generation.

The intelligence layer

Research that took years now takes minutes and hours.

Built into every Iho portal are intelligent research tools calibrated to the specific complexity of whanau trust governance. This is not a generic tool. It was built for your trust's real problems.

Grants Finder
Scans all available funding relevant to your trust's specific profile, assets and location.
Lands Finder
Identifies your land blocks, ownership shares, succession interests and unclaimed holdings.
Succession Advisor
Guides the succession process step by step and flags unclaimed interests and overdue applications.
Document Drafter
Produces trustee reports, Maori Land Court submissions and governance documents ready to send.
Research Tool
Searches court records, historical data and whakapapa connections in minutes not months.

Proof of concept live on the ITTS Whanau Trust. Research that stalled for decades was completed in days. This is not a concept. It works.

For years I watched our whanau trust struggle with no transparency, no accessible records, no guidance on available support. Critical knowledge lived in one kaumatua's memory. When she passed, years of history went with her.

So I built the solution for our own ITTS Whanau Trust first. What previous trustees had spent years trying to untangle, including succession matters, unclaimed dividends and coastal land research, was completed in days using Iho's intelligent tools.

Iho is now available to every whanau trust in Aotearoa that needs it. From the core. For generations.
