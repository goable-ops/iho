Te Taiao Motuhake

Have you and your whānau ever stood on your whenua and wondered what it could become?

The feeling is real. The vision is there. But then comes the question that stops most whānau in their tracks.

Where do we even start?

You start with the whare.

Before anything else, the whare is the foundation. It is where your whānau gathers, where decisions are made, where the plan gets built. Get the foundation right and everything else becomes possible.

Your digital wharē is that foundation. The central hub from which the whole picture gets mapped, resourced and built. One step at a time, at your pace, with your whānau leading every decision.

He aha te digital wharē

What is a
digital wharē?

A digital wharē is your whānau trust's private online home. A secure place that brings everything together. Accessible to every whānau member, wherever they are in the world.

Think of it as the marae of your digital world. A place to gather, to share, to preserve and to act. Built around the specific needs of your trust and your whānau.

Understand your land

Your trust's land blocks, shares and succession laid out clearly. Know exactly what your whānau owns, where it is and what needs to happen with it.

Lands research
Find what you may be owed

Unclaimed dividends, succession entitlements and other benefits that may have been sitting untouched for years. Our research tools help surface what most trusts never knew existed.

Unclaimed interests
Explore funding sources

Your digital wharē includes a built-in grants research tool that helps your trust identify and explore funding sources that may be relevant to your situation and goals.

Grants research
Reconnect your whānau

Whānau living away stay connected through your private portal. Every decision, every update, every opportunity shared in one trusted space. No one left out of the loop.

Connection
Preserve your history

Your whakapapa, stories, photographs and land history kept safe for every generation ahead. What your kaumātua carry in memory. Recorded, protected and passed on.

Whakapapa
Help whānau get online

Your portal can surface local digital access support for whānau who are not yet connected. Programmes that help people get online exist in many regions. Your wharē helps surface what is available near your whānau.

Digital access

This model grows with you. Everything built for a whānau trust can extend to a marae, a hapū or an iwi. The foundations are the same. The application is yours to shape.

The reality

These are challenges
many whānau trusts
know well.

Iho was built because we lived these challenges from the inside of our own whānau trust. If any of this sounds familiar. You are not alone.

🙈
No transparency

Whānau have no visibility of what the trust owns, what decisions are made, or where the money goes. Over time trust breaks down.

📡
Whānau disconnected

Important news travels by word of mouth or not at all. Beneficiaries living away feel cut off from their own trust and their own land.

💸
Money no one knows about

Unclaimed dividends, succession entitlements and funding sources that no one has applied for. Resources sitting untouched for years.

🧠
Knowledge that disappears

The trust's history lives in one kaumātua's memory. When they pass, decades of whakapapa and knowledge goes with them.

📦
No proper records

Documents, court orders and minutes scattered across shoeboxes, inboxes and old hard drives. Nothing in one place, nothing backed up.

🌱
Whenua with untapped potential

Many trusts hold whenua that could be doing more for their whānau. Without the right information and tools, it is hard to even know where to begin.

Sound familiar? That is exactly why Iho was built. Register your interest and be part of what comes next.

Our story
"I am building this for our own whānau and whenua first. Because the only honest way to bring something to your people is to prove it works at home. That is what Iho is. Built from the inside out, with aroha."

Donna SmithFounder, Iho. Trustee, whānau trust. Ngāi Te Rangi, Tauwhao hapū.

Iho was created from my personal journey of reconnecting with my whānau, my whenua, my whakapapa, my culture and my people. I know these challenges. I have lived them. I watched our trust struggle for years with no accessible records, no way to keep whānau informed, no guidance on what support was available or what our land held. So I built a digital wharē for our own trust first. Now I want to bring it to as many whānau trusts across Aotearoa as we can.
