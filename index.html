The challenges

Every whanau trust faces the same walls.

5,400 trusts. Most still running on paper, phone calls and memory. Iho was built because we lived these problems from the inside of our own ITTS Whanau Trust.

No transparency
Whanau have no visibility of what the trust owns, what decisions are made, or what money comes in. Trust breaks down.
Whanau disconnected
Important news travels by word of mouth or not at all. Beneficiaries living away feel cut off from their own trust.
Hidden money
Unclaimed dividends sitting with agencies for years. Grants available that nobody applies for. Money the trust never knows it has.
Knowledge that disappears
The trust's history lives in one kaumatua's memory. When they pass, decades of knowledge and whakapapa goes with them.
No proper records
Land documents, court orders and meeting minutes scattered across shoeboxes, inboxes and old computers with no backup.
Land left unused
Whanau trusts hold valuable whenua but lack the tools to understand it, manage it or build economic opportunity from it.
What's inside your whare

Everything your trust needs in one place.

Every Iho portal is completely private and isolated. Only your trustees and approved whanau can access it. Designed to be simple for all ages — from kaumatua to rangatahi.

Whakapapa Records
Your family history preserved and accessible for every generation. Never lost, never forgotten. Safe for your tamariki and their tamariki.
Land Holdings Dashboard
A clear view of all your trust's land blocks, ownership shares and succession status. Know exactly what your whanau owns and where it is.
Document Vault
Trust orders, land titles, court documents and historical papers stored safely online. Find anything in seconds — never hunt through paper files again.
Governance Hub
Meeting minutes, trustee resolutions and annual reports in one searchable record. A complete transparent history of how your trust has been run.
Korero and Whakaaro
A private space for trustees and whanau to share news, announcements and ideas. Everyone stays connected no matter where in the world they live.
Photos and Taonga
A gallery to preserve whanau photographs, cultural taonga and memories across time. Your story held safely for the generations still to come.

For years I watched our whanau trust struggle with no accessible records, no way to keep whanau informed and no guidance on what support was available. Important knowledge lived in our kaumatua's memory alone.

So I built a digital whare for our own ITTS Whanau Trust. What previous trustees had spent years trying to resolve — succession matters, unclaimed dividends, land research — was completed in days using Iho's tools.

Iho is now available to every whanau trust in Aotearoa. Built by a trustee, for trustees. And built to help your whanau access everything your tupuna always intended for you.
