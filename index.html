A self-sustaining
ecosystem built on

Te Taiao Motuhake — a living ecosystem rooted in mana motuhake and kaitiakitanga. Not handed down from outside. Built by your whānau, from the inside out.

We are proving this model on our own land first — the ITTS Whānau Trust, Atififi. Once proven, it belongs to every trust in Aotearoa.

We are not inventing something new. We are returning to something true — with modern tools in our hands.

MuaThe past

Your tūpuna knew how

They grew kai, harvested kaimoana, lived on the whenua and passed knowledge through doing. The land and the people were one and the same. That knowledge is still alive.

ĀianeiThe present

The land is there. The kaimoana is there. The maunga is standing. What has been missing is the structure, the connection and the tools to bring it all back to life.

A living ecosystem. A complete digital record of every land block, every story, every decision. Employment, kai, connection and identity — built now, for them.

He aha tā Iho

Your digital wharē
is the iho of your
whole ecosystem.

A thriving whenua ecosystem needs a digital layer — somewhere everything is visible, connected and accessible to every whānau member, wherever they are.

Find your land

All your trust's land blocks, shares and succession clearly laid out. Know exactly what your whānau owns and where it is.

Access what you are owed

Unclaimed dividends, succession rights and benefits sitting untouched for years — surfaced by our research tools.

Unlock grant funding

Our grants finder identifies every source your trust is eligible for — Te Puni Kōkiri, Poutama Trust, MWDI and more.

Reconnect your whānau

Beneficiaries anywhere in the world stay connected through your private portal. News, decisions and history in one trusted space.

Preserve your history

Your whakapapa, stories, photographs and land history kept safe for every generation ahead. What your kaumātua carry in memory — preserved forever.

Get every whānau online

Resources like Skinny Jump surfaced so no whānau member is left offline and disconnected. Five dollars, 35GB, no contract.

This model extends further. Everything built for a whānau trust can be applied to a marae, a hapū or an iwi. The foundations are the same — the application is yours to shape.

The reality

Every whānau trust
faces the same walls.

5,400 trusts. Most still running on paper, phone calls and memory. Iho was built because we lived these problems from the inside of our own ITTS Whānau Trust.

🙈
No transparency

Whānau have no visibility of what the trust owns, what decisions are made, or what money comes in. Trust breaks down.

📡
Whānau disconnected

Important news travels by word of mouth or not at all. Beneficiaries living away feel cut off from their own trust.

💸
Hidden money

Unclaimed dividends sitting with agencies for years. Grants nobody applies for. Money the trust never knows it has.

🧠
Knowledge that disappears

The trust's history lives in one kaumātua's memory. When they pass, decades of whakapapa goes with them.

📦
No proper records

Land documents, court orders and meeting minutes scattered across shoeboxes, inboxes and old computers.

🌱
Land left unused

Whānau trusts hold valuable whenua but lack the tools to understand it, manage it or build opportunity from it.

Our story
"I am building this for our own whānau and whenua first. Because the only honest way to bring something to your people is to prove it works at home. That is what Iho is — built from the inside out, with aroha."

Donna Smith Founder, Iho. Trustee, ITTS Whānau Trust. Ngāi Te Rangi, Tauwhao hapū.

Iho was created from my personal journey of reconnecting with my whānau, my whenua, my whakapapa, my culture and my people. I know the challenges. I understand the responsibility. I watched our trust struggle for years with no accessible records, no way to keep whānau informed, no guidance on what support was available. So I built a digital wharē for our own trust first. Now I want every trust in Aotearoa to have what we built.
