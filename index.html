Is your whanau trust struggling to keep everyone connected?

If your trust is managing land records on paper, making decisions by email, or losing important history when an elder passes — you are not alone. Every whanau trust in Aotearoa faces the same challenges. Iho was built to solve them, simply and affordably.

"Iho gives your trust a private digital home. A safe place to store your whakapapa, your land records, your history and your documents — all in one place, easy to find, never lost."
The challenges

Every whanau trust faces the same walls.

5,400 trusts across Aotearoa. Most still running on paper, phone calls and hope. Iho was built because we lived these problems from inside our own trust.

No transparency
Whanau members have no visibility of what the trust owns, what decisions are being made, or what money has come in.
No communication
Whanau are disconnected from their own trust. Important news travels by word of mouth or not at all.
Hidden money
Unclaimed dividends sitting with agencies for years. Nobody knows they exist because nobody has looked.
Knowledge loss
The history of the trust lives in one kaumatua's memory. When they pass, decades of knowledge goes with them.
No proper records
Land documents, meeting minutes and legal records are scattered across shoeboxes, emails and old computers.
Missing out on grants
There is money available for whanau trusts that most trusts never know about or never get around to applying for.
What's inside your whare

Everything your trust needs. In one place.

Iho gives your whanau trust a private, secure digital home. Every section is designed to be simple and easy to use — for trustees, elders and younger whanau alike.

Whakapapa Records
Keep your family history alive and accessible for every generation. Your whakapapa preserved in one safe place for your tamariki and mokopuna.
Land Holdings
A clear dashboard showing all your trust's land blocks, ownership shares and succession status. No more hunting through paper files.
Document Vault
Store trust orders, land titles, court correspondence and all your important documents safely online. Find anything in seconds.
Governance Hub
Meeting minutes, trustee resolutions and annual reports all in one searchable place. A complete record of how your trust has been run.
Korero and Whakaaro
A private space for your trustees and whanau to share news, make announcements and stay connected no matter where they live.
Photos and Taonga
A gallery to preserve and share whanau photographs, memories and cultural taonga. Your story kept safe for the generations ahead.
The research tools

Research that took years now takes a few hours.

Built inside every Iho portal are intelligent tools that do the hard research work for you. These tools were built specifically for the complexity of whanau trust governance — and tested on our own ITTS Trust first.

Grants Finder
Finds all available funding that your specific trust could apply for
Lands Finder
Identifies your land blocks, shares and any unclaimed interests
Succession Advisor
Guides you through succession step by step and flags what needs action
Document Drafter
Writes trustee reports and court submissions ready to send
Research Tool
Searches court records and whakapapa connections in minutes not months

These tools are already live on the ITTS Whanau Trust. Research that had stalled for decades was completed in days. This is not a promise — it is a proven result.
