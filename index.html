Te Taiao Motuhake

A self-sustaining whānau ecosystem
built on your own whenua.

Te Taiao Motuhake — a living ecosystem rooted in mana motuhake (self-determination) and kaitiakitanga (guardianship). Not handed down from outside. Built by your whānau, from the inside out. We are proving this model on our own land first — the ITTS Whānau Trust, Atififi. Once proven, it belongs to every trust in Aotearoa.

Ko ahau te taiao, ko te taiao, ko ahau — the ecosystem and I are one.

Hono i mua, hono i muri

Connecting the past, the present
and the future.

We are not inventing something new. We are returning to something true — with modern tools in our hands.

Mua — the past
Your tūpuna knew how

They grew kai, harvested kaimoana, lived on the whenua and passed knowledge through doing. The land and the people were one and the same. That knowledge is still alive in us.

Āianei — the present
The whenua is still yours

The land is there. The kaimoana is there. The maunga is standing. What has been missing is the structure, the connection and the tools to bring it all back to life. That is what Iho provides.

Āmuri ake — the future
Your mokopuna inherit something whole

A living ecosystem. A complete digital record of every land block, every story, every decision. Employment, kai, connection and identity — built now, for them.

He aha tā Iho

Your digital wharē is the iho
of your whole ecosystem.

A thriving whenua ecosystem needs a digital layer — somewhere everything is visible, connected and accessible to every whānau member, wherever they are in the world.

🗺️
Find your land

All your trust's land blocks, shares and succession clearly laid out. Know exactly what your whānau owns and where it is.

💰
Access what you are owed

Unclaimed dividends, succession rights and benefits sitting untouched for years. Our tools surface what most trusts never knew existed.

📋
Unlock grant funding

Our grants finder identifies every source your trust is eligible for — Te Puni Kōkiri, Poutama Trust, MWDI and more. Then we help you apply.

🔗
Reconnect your whānau

Beneficiaries anywhere in the world stay connected through your portal. News, decisions and history shared in one private, trusted space.

📚
Preserve your history

Your whakapapa, stories, photographs and land history kept safe for every generation ahead. What your kaumātua carry in memory — preserved forever.

🌐
Get every whānau online

Resources like Skinny Jump surfaced so no whānau member is left offline. Five dollars, 35GB, no contract.

This model extends further

Everything built for a whānau trust can be applied to a marae, a hapū or an iwi. The digital wharē grows with your community — the foundations are the same, the application is yours to shape.

How it works

You do not need to find
the money first.

Most whānau trusts cannot invest in digital tools from their own pocket. Iho solves that. We guide your trust through finding and applying for the grant that pays for everything.

01
We find your grant

We identify and apply for funding that covers your Iho setup and first year. Your trust pays nothing upfront.

02
We build your wharē

Once funded, we build your private digital wharē — customised with your whakapapa, land, documents and history.

Built for you
03

Your portal finds more grants, surfaces land interests, manages succession and builds economic opportunity on your whenua.

Our story
"I am building this for our own whānau and whenua first — the ITTS Whānau Trust. Because the only honest way to bring something to your people is to prove it works at home. That is what Iho is — built from the inside out, with aroha."

Donna Smith
Founder, Iho. Trustee, ITTS Whānau Trust.
Ngāi Te Rangi, Tauwhao hapū. Aotearoa New Zealand.

Iho was created from my personal journey of reconnecting with my whānau, my whenua, my whakapapa, my culture and my people. I know the challenges. I understand the responsibility. And I believe there is a better way. I watched our trust struggle for years with no accessible records, no way to keep whānau informed and no guidance on what support was available. So I built a digital wharē for our own trust first. Now I want every trust in Aotearoa to have what we built.
